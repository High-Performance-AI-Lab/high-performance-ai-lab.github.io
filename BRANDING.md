# High Performance AI Lab — brand

The public name is **High Performance AI Lab**. The short form is **HPAL**.
Site identity is held centrally in `src/consts.ts`.

## Positioning

High Performance AI Lab builds open systems for local inference, proofs, and measured intelligence — from task to silicon.

## Voice and tone guardrails

Whatever the name, the copy should avoid:
- "Revolutionize," "unlock," "leverage," "synergize," "supercharge."
- Vague claims about "the future of AI."
- Gradient text and glow as a substitute for substance.
- Claims you have not measured.

Instead, use:
- Concrete verbs: run, bind, prove, measure, fit, restore, observe, craft.
- Named conditions: device, model, quant, route, seed, trials.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Honesty about limits and failures.

## Website copy rules

Copy is read by humans deciding whether to trust us. Beyond the voice
guardrails above:

- Lead with why a thing exists and what it is for. Numbers come second,
  with their conditions.
- Never write copy about the copy. No text that references launch wording,
  internal review, governance documents, or its own honesty machinery.
  The reader gets the discipline's results, not its narration.
- No time-anchored copy. "Today" and "this month" go stale; write sentences
  that are still true next year.
- Section titles are plain. Not every heading needs to be memorable.
- Protocol and security specifics live in READMEs, not on landing pages.
- Every claim about people is literally true: no implied collaborations,
  no invented track record, check the geography.
- Never name unreleased or private work on any public surface, including
  commit messages.
